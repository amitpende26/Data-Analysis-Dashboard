# Data-Analysis-Dashboard
📊 Transforming HR Analytics into Interactive Dashboards
📥 Dashboard Preview (PDF)

Create Power BI Dashboard from HR Attrition Dataset
This project focuses on converting raw HR data into an insightful and interactive Power BI dashboard. It aims to help stakeholders analyze employee attrition, understand workforce behavior, and monitor HR performance metrics to support strategic decisions.

🧩 Problem Statement
"Develop a Power BI dashboard to visualize and analyze employee attrition data, enabling HR teams to gain actionable insights into employee behavior, performance metrics, and workforce trends. The dashboard is designed to enhance decision-making in areas like retention, diversity, departmental efficiency, and compensation."

🔍 Key Aspects Covered
Workforce Overview

Visuals include: employee count, average age, years at company, and salary overview.

Quick glance at overall workforce health and composition.

Attrition Analysis

Attrition metrics based on age group, gender, education level, and job role.

Understand who is leaving and why.

Departmental & Role-Based Breakdown

Track attrition by job role and department to identify high-risk zones.

Helps in focusing retention efforts where they matter most.

Salary & Tenure Insights

Correlation between attrition and salary brackets.

Relationship between years spent in the company and employee churn.

Demographic Segmentation

Filter employee data by gender, education, and department.

Gain a deeper understanding of your employee base for better HR strategies.

📁 Dataset Details
Variable	Description
Age	Employee's age
Department	Department of employment
JobRole	Role/title of employee
MonthlyIncome	Salary (monthly income)
Attrition	Whether the employee left the company
Education	Education level (categorical)
Gender	Gender of the employee
YearsAtCompany	Tenure (in years)
TotalWorkingYears	Total years of professional experience
And other HR-related attributes…

🧹 How the Dashboard Was Built
Data Cleaning

Removed missing/null entries

Formatted columns for Power BI compatibility

Categorical encodings adjusted for better visual grouping

Data Transformation

Created new calculated columns (e.g., total tenure, salary bins, etc.)

Grouped values for meaningful segmentation in visuals

Visualization in Power BI

Created interactive bar charts, pie charts, slicers, KPIs, and comparative cards

Used DAX for measures like attrition rate, average salary, and headcount
